# FinOptima: An End-to-End Statistical Portfolio & Credit Risk Engine

## 1. Executive Summary
The "Black Box" problem in Fintech is a massive industry pain point. Modern AI models often lack the statistical transparency required by regulators such as Basel III/IV. FinOptima is designed to solve this by building a transparent, mathematically rigorous engine for asset allocation and risk assessment. By leveraging pure Probability, Statistics, and Probability & Statistical Learning (PSPL) concepts, FinOptima provides an interpretable, end-to-end solution for modern financial institutions.

## 2. Core Objectives
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Build models that align with Basel III/IV transparency requirements.
- **Statistical Rigor:** Replace opaque AI with interpretable PSPL models.
- **End-to-End Implementation:** Develop a complete pipeline from data ingestion to risk assessment and portfolio optimization.

## 3. Core Modules
- **Module 1: Market Risk & Default Probability Engine:** Bayesian credit scoring and asset pricing distributions.
- **Module 2: Portfolio Correlation & Stress Testing:** Covariance matrices, VaR calculations, and CLT stress testing.
- **Module 3: Predictive Credit Modeling & Alpha Generation:** Logistic/Linear regression for default prediction and factor models.
- **Module 4: Optimal Asset Allocation:** Portfolio optimization using LPP and the Simplex method.

## 4. Technical Stack
- **Language:** Python 3.10+
- **Data:** Pandas, NumPy
- **Stats:** SciPy, Statsmodels, Scikit-learn
- **Optimization:** scipy.optimize.linprog, PuLP
